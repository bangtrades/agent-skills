## One sentence

A warm late-modern civic interior, seen from a level above, with colour on the floor and light in the roof.

## Rules

- Era: late-modern civic and corporate architecture, 1935–1980 look; "1960s–70s architectural photography, colour transparency film." Not glass-and-steel minimalism, loft, or Scandinavian oak.
- Motif, one per image, in priority: solid curved parapet (the signature; reads as a mezzanine) → ribbing (glass tubes, ribbed timber, mullions) → stair as diagonal → round or oval opening.
- Colour low, light high: saturated red or terracotta on the floor plane, cream plaster structure, deep brown shadow, brightest zone at the roof. Ask for red **material** (carpet, lacquer, textile); generators refuse red light. Never a red ceiling or blue floor. Type sits on the cream zone, not on carpet.
- One small cool plane (sky, skylight) allowed at the top edge, under 10% of frame. Planting optional.
- Two lighting modes, never mixed: daylit cream (~70%, hero, services, methodology) and evening amber (~30%, The Upper Deck, contact). At most two amber images per page.
- Viewpoint: from the level above looking down and across (most Mezzanine), on-axis corridor or stair, or close detail of one curve. 28–40 mm equivalent; 50–85 mm for detail. Verticals controlled, never fisheye.
- People: zero to three, none over 5% of frame height, none facing camera. Founder photo is Gillian's real photo (`assets/motion/founder-gillian-1810x2717.png`, crop 2:3).
- Colour is the default. B&W is a CSS or layout treatment; keep colour in the source. Wide slots need new 16:9 or 2.35:1 compositions; do not crop portraits. Reserve 30–40% of one side as quiet plaster for type-bearing heroes.
- Reject: red ceiling, blue floor, balusters as main figure, stairs to nowhere, mixed lighting, faces, contemporary minimalism, uniform orange grade, HDR, fisheye, film border, timestamp, any letters.
- Generation: Higgsfield `cinematic_studio_2_5` for stills, `cinematic_studio_video_v2` for clips; deck photos are Instagram saves and never ship.
