# Spreadsheets (.xlsx, comp guides, trackers, models)

Read `xlsx` skill for mechanics. Spreadsheets are working tools: keep them quiet and readable; brand shows in type, one red accent and the header block.

## Sheet frame

Row 1: `MEZZANINE` Söhne (or Arial) 9 caps ink, letter-spaced with a trailing space per character if the app cannot track. Row 2: sheet title Austin (or Georgia) 16 Roman. Row 3: eyebrow, date, owner in 8 caps `#6E6862`. Row 4 blank. Freeze panes below the header row. Gridlines off; use 0.5 pt `#DDD5C9` borders on the data range only.

## Table

Header row: fill ink `#141313`, text paper `#EBE5DD` 9 caps Söhne Halbfett (Arial Bold fallback), 24 px tall. Body 10 Söhne Buch / Arial. Zebra: none; use `#F4F0EA` for subtotal rows only. Totals: 0.5 pt red top rule, Halbfett. Numbers right-aligned, `#,##0`; currency `$#,##0`; percentages `0.0%`; dates `d MMM yyyy`.

## Colour

Positive/negative: never green/red; use ink and `#6E6862`, with red only for the one figure the reader must see. Conditional formatting: paper `#EBE5DD` fill for flagged cells, red 0.5 pt left border for action items. Inputs: `#F4F0EA` fill with ink text. Charts: series ink, red, orange, `#6E6862`; gridlines `#DDD5C9`; no 3D, no smoothing, square markers.

## Logo

Print header: wordmark PNG (ink) 1.6 in wide top-left, or none. No logo inside the grid. Tab colour red for the cover or summary sheet only.
