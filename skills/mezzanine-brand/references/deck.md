# Presentations (.pptx, Keynote, Google Slides)

Read `pptx` skill for mechanics. Deck rhythm follows the guideline PDF itself: 16:10 or 16:9, paper pages with red divider slides.

## Masters

- Cover: full red `#C72A09`, ink wordmark PNG bottom-left 45% wide, title Söhne Halbfett caps top-left 20 pt, ©year small.
- Divider: full red, section word Söhne Halbfett caps 40 pt ink top-left, nav list bottom-right 8 pt with arrow on the current item.
- Content: paper `#EBE5DD` ground, 8 pt Söhne caps running heads (left: deck name, right: section), footer `©YYYY · MEZZANINE · n`.
- Statement: paper or ink ground, one Austin line 44–60 pt, nothing else.
- Dark: ink ground, paper text, Austin display in red for one phrase.
- Closing: red, one Söhne Halbfett caps statement, icon ink 0.9 in bottom-right.

## Type (points, 16:9 at 13.33 in)

Slide title 28–32 Austin Roman · statement 44–60 Austin Light or Roman, lh 0.95–1.05 · body 14–16 Söhne Buch lh 1.3 · bullets 14 Söhne, use sparingly, prefer short paragraphs · caption 9 · eyebrow 9 caps +0.1 em. Fonts are installed on the Mac (`Austin`, `Söhne`); embed on export. Georgia / Arial only for recipients without them.

## Rules

One red slide in every 4–6. Never red text on red. Ink on red only at ≥ 24 pt. Charts: series 1 ink, 2 red, 3 orange, 4 `#6E6862`; gridlines `#DDD5C9`; no 3D, no gradients. Images: full-bleed or in a square-cornered frame with 0.5 pt rule; B&W allowed as treatment. Logos: wordmark on cover and closing only; icon as the corner sign-off on content slides at 0.4 in, never smaller than 42 px on screen. Speaker notes in Söhne.
