# Copy Archetypes

These are structures, not pre-approved claims. Replace the facts with sourced, current, approved evidence.

## Homepage hero

**Eyebrow:** Healthcare insurance and risk advisory

**Headline:** See the whole risk program before you choose the renewal path.

**Body:** James & Gable brings insurance structure, claims experience, clinical and employee safety, and risk financing into one objective review—supported by Trucordia’s broader market access and infrastructure.

**CTA:** Request a program review

**Trust line:** A licensed James & Gable professional will review your request. Coverage and placement are subject to underwriting and market terms.

## Service-page opening

**Headline:** Insurance brokerage built around the decisions healthcare organizations actually face.

**Body pattern:** Start with the situation. Name the dimensions evaluated. Explain the output. Identify the licensed-human next step.

**Example:** A renewal is more than a premium comparison. We evaluate loss experience, coverage, limits, retention, and market options, then work with your team to develop and implement a clear placement strategy.

## Benefit triplet

1. **Understand the current program** — Identify coverage, claims, data, and structure issues before market engagement.
2. **Compare realistic options** — Evaluate transfer, retention, and financing alternatives with their tradeoffs visible.
3. **Act through one accountable team** — Coordinate placement, implementation, and year-round risk work with licensed advisors.

## Insight headline and deck

**Headline formula:** `[Healthcare risk change]: what [specific leader] should review before [decision point]`

**Example:** Property capacity is changing: what hospital risk leaders should review before renewal.

**Deck:** Separate the market signal from the client action. Include date, affected segment, and data source.

## Case study

**Context:** organization type, program scope, and decision—not the client name unless approved.

**Challenge:** the observable risk, market, claims, or governance issue.

**Evaluation:** data and options considered.

**Action:** what the client and advisory team implemented.

**Outcome:** measured result, period, scope, and limitations.

**Disclosure:** client-specific results are not a guarantee of future outcomes; coverage and market terms vary.

## Proposal slide

**Title:** Decision required: professional and general liability structure

**Current position:** three sourced bullets.

**Options:** transfer, retention, financing, each with cost/volatility/governance implications.

**Recommendation:** one sentence plus rationale.

**Next step:** named owner, action, and date.

**Footer:** data period, sources, assumptions, and licensing/underwriting qualifier.

## Email subject and opening

**Subject:** Items to resolve before the healthcare liability renewal

**Opening:** We completed the first review of your loss history, current structure, and market information. Three decisions need your team’s input before we finalize the renewal strategy.

## LinkedIn post

**Observation:** one specific market or risk signal.

**Implication:** why it matters to a named healthcare role.

**Action:** one question or checklist item.

**Close:** link to the full sourced note or invite a conversation with a licensed advisor.

**Example:** A lower quoted rate does not necessarily mean a lower long-term cost of risk. Before renewal, compare the retention, coverage changes, loss assumptions, and governance demands behind each option. Our new checklist shows the questions a hospital finance and risk team should resolve together.

## Press-release lead
