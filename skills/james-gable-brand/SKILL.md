# James & Gable Brand

Use this skill to produce work that feels like a healthcare-specialist advisor: composed, specific, relationship-led, and accountable. Preserve James & Gable’s blue institutional identity while making Trucordia’s role clear whenever ownership, infrastructure, scale, or technology matters.

## Operating rule

Lead with the client’s healthcare risk decision, not the insurance product. Explain what will be evaluated, why it matters, and what a licensed advisor will do next. Never promise premium savings, claim reduction, coverage, placement, or an underwriting outcome.

## Brand core

**Role:** healthcare insurance and risk advisor.

**Promise:** specialist judgment that connects insurance structure, claims experience, clinical and employee safety, and long-term cost of risk.

**Personality:** experienced, objective, attentive, calm, technically fluent, and direct.

**Relationship to Trucordia:** James & Gable supplies healthcare relevance and relationship continuity; Trucordia supplies broader market access, operating infrastructure, data, and technology. Do not imply that James & Gable is independent of Trucordia.

## Creation workflow

1. Identify the audience and the decision they must make.
2. Select the relevant service and risk-maturity layer from `product-architecture.md`.
3. Classify every factual or quantitative claim using `positioning-and-claims.md`.
4. Draft in the calm, consultative voice defined in `voice-and-tone.md`.
5. Apply the visual hierarchy and tokens in `visual-tokens.md`.
6. Add a clear next step that routes to a licensed professional.
7. Run the release checklist below.

## Release checklist

- The parent/operating-brand relationship is accurate and understandable.
- “Serving healthcare since 1991” is not silently converted to “founded in 1991.”
- Quantitative claims have a source, date, scope, owner, and approval.
- Client names, logos, outcomes, and renewal details have written permission.
- Premium, claims, or loss statements are client-specific and appropriately qualified.
- Coverage language does not imply binding, guarantee, or universal applicability.
- AI-supported experiences disclose the licensed-human decision point.
- No PHI, confidential loss detail, or sensitive personal data appears in prompts or marketing assets.
- The next action is useful and low-friction; it does not rely on urgency theater.
- The result uses Merriweather/Montserrat, James & Gable blue, white space, and institutional healthcare imagery or approved evidence graphics.
