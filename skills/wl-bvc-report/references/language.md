# BVC report language and disclosure

Preserve client confidentiality and write in plain business language. Anonymize company, people, partners, and product names when needed. Configure `client_terms` to catch accidental disclosure.

State actual maturity: prototype, demonstration, pilot, or production. Disclose simulated, synthetic, or real data and the scope of any integration. Do not convert a fictional-data demo into a claim of production deployment or measured business results.

Label savings and conventional-team comparisons as estimates with method and assumptions. Separate measured outcomes, observed build facts, and projected value. Confirm claims against build/deployment evidence; file mtimes alone do not measure effort.

The generator permits these disclosures by default and still rejects configured confidential client terms. Custom `forbidden_terms` must not suppress required provenance or maturity information. Inspect extracted artifact text and rendered pages before delivery.
