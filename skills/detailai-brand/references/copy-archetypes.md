# DetailAI — Copy Archetypes

Reusable patterns, calibrated on observed site copy. Substitute the {braces}; keep the rhythm.

## 1. Hero statement
Eyebrow (uppercase, small) + giant serif line + quantified sub.
> CONCOURS GRADE SOFTWARE FOR DETAILERS
> **Every lead answered. Quote in 90 seconds.**
> A call hits voicemail? DetailAI texts back in seconds, classifies the vehicle, and quotes from your menu — at your prices.

Pattern: `{EYEBROW: category claim}` / `{Two clipped sentences: outcome + speed number}` / `{One mechanism sentence ending "at your prices" or equivalent ownership clause}`

## 2. Stakes headline (problem section)
> The $2,000 job goes to whoever answers first.

Pattern: `The {dollar-figure} {noun} goes to {whoever does X first}.` — money + urgency without exclamation.

## 3. Craft-dignity manifesto line
> The best detailers are artists. They should be perfecting paint, not apologizing for missed texts.

Pattern: `The best {craftsmen} are {artists/identity}. They should be {craft verb}, not {admin indignity}.`

## 4. Ownership/training line
> One agent. Trained on your shop.

Pattern: fragment + fragment. `{Singular thing}. {Trained/built} on your {shop/menu/voice}.`

## 5. Mirror headline (upsell)
> Your work is concours. Your website isn't.

Pattern: `Your {craft output} is {premium standard}. Your {business surface} isn't.`

## 6. Pricing frame
> Two ways in. No tiers, no meters.
> One extra $2,000 job pays for four months.

Pattern: counted simplicity + ROI arithmetic. Never list features per tier.

## 7. FAQ answer style ("Asked, answered.")
One-sentence direct answer first, one sentence of mechanism, stop. Include honesty guardrails where quoting is involved ("Nothing is estimated or made up.").

## 8. CTA labels
"Request private demo" · "Book a private demo" · "Quote yourself, as a customer" — always private/personal register. Never "Start free trial," "Get started," "Learn more."

## 9. SMS agent voice (in-product)
> Hi — this is {Shop}'s assistant. Sorry we missed your call. What vehicle are we looking at, and what are you hoping to have done?
Then: classify → quote from menu → "Want me to hold {day/time} for you? {Owner} will confirm."
Plain, warm, fast. No brand poetry inside the SMS thread.

## 10. Founding-shop outreach (email)
Subject: short, lowercase-adjacent, specific ("your missed calls, answered"). Body: 3 short paragraphs — observed problem in their world, what the agent does in one sentence with a number, private-demo invitation signed by a named human. No bullets, no images.

## 11. Recovered-revenue proof block (dashboards/case studies)
> {N} after-hours leads answered. {N} quotes sent. {$X} booked.
Gold numerals, cream labels, stone captions. The number is the design.
