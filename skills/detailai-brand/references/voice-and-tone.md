# DetailAI — Voice & Tone

## Voice descriptors

Atelier-premium · terse · confident · craft-reverent · anti-generic · concierge-exclusive

## The register

Write like a master detailer talks about paint: short declarative sentences, specific numbers, zero hype adjectives. The brand respects the customer's craft and mirrors it in software. Think concours program notes, not SaaS landing page.

Real examples (verbatim from the site — the calibration set):

- "Every lead answered. Quote in 90 seconds."
- "Concours grade software for detailers"
- "The $2,000 job goes to whoever answers first."
- "One agent. Trained on your shop."
- "Your work is concours. Your website isn't."
- "Two ways in. No tiers, no meters."
- "Asked, answered."
- "The best detailers are artists. They should be perfecting paint, not apologizing for missed texts."
- "Built for studios doing $2,000 ceramic coatings, not $39 washes."

## Rules

1. **Sentence economy.** Headlines ≤8 words. Two short sentences beat one long one. Period-heavy rhythm ("Asked, answered.").
2. **Numbers do the persuading.** Seconds, dollars, minutes. "Quotes in 90 seconds," "one extra $2,000 job pays for four months." Never "blazingly fast," "revolutionary," "game-changing."
3. **Craft vocabulary.** studio, shop, menu, agent, concours, paint correction, ceramic, PPF, white-glove. The customer runs a *studio*, not a *business*; they have a *menu*, not a *price list*.
4. **Verbs of service:** answer, quote, book, classify, text back, train, configure. The agent *answers*; it never "engages," "delights," or "leverages."
5. **Exclusivity, not urgency.** "Private demo," "founding shops only," "two ways in." Never countdown timers, "act now," discounts, or exclamation points.
6. **Honesty guardrails are brand.** Quotes are "from your menu, at your prices," "subject to confirmation by the shop," "nothing is estimated or made up." Keep these in any surface that mentions quoting.
7. **Anti-generic stance.** "No generic chatbot scripts." "Not a widget bolted on." Position against sameness explicitly.
8. **Second person, present tense.** "A call hits voicemail? DetailAI texts back in seconds."
9. **No emoji, no exclamation points, minimal adjectives.** The gold accent is the excitement; the words stay level.

## Tone by surface

| Surface | Tone shift |
|---|---|
| Marketing site / decks | Full atelier register, big serif statements |
| App UI | Terse and utilitarian; serif only for page titles; keep craft nouns |
| SMS (the agent itself) | Warm-professional, plain Inter-world language, shop's own voice; speed + accuracy, no brand poetry |
| Legal / ops docs | Standard plain-language; keep A2P/STOP/HELP compliance language intact |
| Founding-shop outreach | Concierge-personal: "personally," "white-glove," named human sender |
