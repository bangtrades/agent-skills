# DetailAI — Positioning & Claims Discipline

## Position

The AI agent that answers every lead and quotes in seconds — for premium detail studios. Sold on recovered revenue, priced against the $2,000 job ($499/mo), not against $70–110/mo scheduling tools.

## Competitive framing rules

1. **Never build a feature-comparison grid vs Urable ($70–183), OrbisX (~$100), Mobile Tech RX ($39–199), Detail Bookie ($99), Fieldd ($199+).** DetailAI loses a features-per-dollar table by construction. Frame instead: "your CRM schedules the jobs you already won; DetailAI wins the jobs you're currently losing."
2. **Acknowledge coexistence.** DetailAI can sit in front of an existing CRM. The observed market behavior (Urable users bolting on GoHighLevel for lead response) is the proof the category pays for this separately.
3. **Against AI receptionists** (Goodcall, GHL agencies, RingCentral AI): "they answer; they can't price. DetailAI quotes from your menu."
4. **QuoteIQ** is the closest prior art (customer self-quoting + AI estimating) — but its math is property-surface (driveways/roofs), not vehicle condition. Do not name it in customer-facing copy; it matters for internal strategy and SEO/content prioritization only.
5. **Name collision:** Mobile Detail AI (mobiledetail.ai) is an unrelated shipped product in the same vertical. Never reference it; ensure our materials always use "DetailAI" as one word, wordmark-styled, with detailai.ai visible, to train branded search.

## Claims we can make (with their required form)

| Claim | Required form |
|---|---|
| Speed | "seconds" / "90 seconds" / "within a minute" — always a number, never "instantly" alone |
| Accuracy | "from your menu, at your prices"; "Nothing is estimated or made up." |
| Quote status | Always "subject to confirmation by the shop" (also in ToS — legally load-bearing) |
| ROI | "One extra $2,000 job pays for four months" — arithmetic form, tied to list price |
| Availability | "Founding shops only" while pre-launch; drop when self-serve opens |

## Claims we may NOT make

- Response-rate/lost-job statistics (78% respond-first, $2,400 lost job, 5-minute window) **as cited facts** — currently uncited on the site. Either source them properly or present as industry rules-of-thumb ("jobs go to whoever answers first"). Flag to bang: sourcing these is open debt.
- Guarantees of revenue, booking rates, or lead volume. The recovered-revenue ledger reports actuals; marketing never promises them.
- "AI never makes mistakes" or equivalents. The guardrail is shop confirmation, not model perfection.
- Anything implying we serve volume washes, fleets, or general field service.

## Compliance notes (SMS)

A2P/10DLC register: consent language on every lead form ("I agree to receive text messages"), STOP/HELP handling mentioned wherever the SMS program is described, program name "DetailAI." Do not strip this language from forms or legal pages to make them prettier.

## Tagline lockup

Primary: **"Concours grade software for detailers."** Hero pair: **"Every lead answered. Quote in 90 seconds."** Do not coin new taglines without bang's sign-off.
