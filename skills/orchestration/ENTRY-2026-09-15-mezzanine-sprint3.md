- **2026-09-15 · Mezzanine "In Motion" v2 minimal rebuild (orchestrator assets + 1 Sonnet dev + Opus gate + 1 Sonnet fix round + orchestrator smoke; 2 P0, 4 P1, all closed in one round; Lighthouse 72 → 100).** Three learnings. **(a) `overflow: hidden` is a scroll container, and anything that can focus or find-in-page will scroll it.** A pinned horizontal track driven by a transform was corrupted permanently by pressing Tab: the browser scrolled the hidden container to reveal the focused panel, the engine kept composing its transform on top, and nothing ever reset `scrollLeft`. Rule: **a viewport that is positioned by script must be `overflow: clip`, and focus must move the page, not the container** — and the gate's keyboard walk (Tab through every focusable, then scroll back to the start and compare the DOM's geometry to a clean load) is the probe that finds it; a mouse-only gate never will. **(b) A presentational `height=""` attribute defeats `aspect-ratio`.** The dev set `aspect-ratio: 2/3` on an `<img width="1200" height="1800">` and got a 400×1800 box; both axes were definite so the ratio never applied, and `object-fit: cover` hid the error as a plausible-looking crop. The dev's own screenshot showed a grey slab and the dev explained it away as "the top edge of the portrait". Rule: **measure the rendered box of every image slot at every viewport (`getBoundingClientRect` w/h ratio) — a screenshot that looks slightly wrong and has an explanation is a P0 until measured.** **(c) A generation model's aesthetic drift is a gate, not a taste question.** Graded against the written imagery language, three of five first-pass plates failed (no priority motif, inverted colour rule, film border) although each looked handsome on the contact sheet; the second pass was prompted motif-first ("one motif: the ribbing", "solid parapet, no balusters") and passed 4/8. Also: `cinematic_studio_2_5` adds a film border on roughly a quarter of 4:5 outputs — scan edge rows by median luminance and trim before shipping; never trust a corner sample. And the dev's Lighthouse "99" came from a Node-API runner that silently kept mobile throttling under a desktop label; the gate's CLI `--preset=desktop` read 72, the fix round's 100. **Rule: report the invocation with the score; a score without its throttling config is a claim about the runner.** Environment: sandbox recycled between turns again (rebuilt Chromium as Chrome-for-Testing 153, which DOES decode H.264, so the video path was finally verified in-sandbox); `/sessions` at 100% — `HOME=/tmp/qahome` required for Chrome's crash handler; QA deps split into `qa/package.json` held up.
