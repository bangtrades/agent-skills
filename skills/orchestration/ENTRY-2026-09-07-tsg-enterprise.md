# What this research wave taught

GPT-5.6 Sol producers researched investment, portfolio, consumer, technology and governance slices. Root maintained the frozen contract, organization anchors, source audit, cross-track semantics, opportunities and final views. Fresh Sol review and bounded repair agents worked on disjoint paths. Firecrawl supplied primary search/scrape retrievals; Tavily was used when exact sites were blocked or thin. Current obligations were checked against controlling originals, with a fresh independent court retrieval correcting an entity’s source gap.

The costly failure was accepting researcher summaries of their own quality. Early drafts reused generic procedure paragraphs and source lists. Lexical deduplication could be defeated accidentally by inserting the entity name into an otherwise repeated paragraph; only reading the mechanism exposed the thinness. Later generic evaluation paragraphs also needed replacement with operation-specific failure cases. The durable lesson is to inspect representative mechanism prose and source entailment before scaling an authoring pattern.

Exact source joins matter, but are insufficient alone. A successful response may be a soft 404 or generic welcome page. The audit now checks actual page identity, HTTP and content error signals, source paths and frozen bytes, with 11 negative controls. Independent review still had to determine whether a retained page actually supported OAuth, object catalogues, rate limits, current court disposition or a whole workflow. The original source title or URL slug cannot substitute for content.

Confidence calibration changed the verdict. All 36 investment and 38 consumer notes contained useful proposed operating specifications whose complete sequences, roles, tests and schemas were not prescribed by their sources. They were relabeled inferred/medium. The final graph has 216 entities and 911 edges; its primary-evidence share 16.7% and high-confidence share 24.5% fail the frozen 35%/55% floors. This is preserved as a qualified research result, not hidden behind the passing source-join and offline checks. A future run must add substantive evidence or use a newly authorized scope rather than rewrite this run’s immutable contract.

The richer staged TSG brand-recon, Cognitive Core S4, vault repair and other prior entries were preserved. This run adds a changelog reconfirmation, not a new law.
