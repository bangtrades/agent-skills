# Orchestration skill — staged changelog entry

- **2026-09-17 · Taste research branch, wave 1 (claude.ai chat, single orchestrator, no sub-agents).** Environment lesson: in the claude.ai chat surface, `firecrawl_agent` (autonomous research runs) requires an interactive approval that may never arrive ("No approval received"), while authenticated `firecrawl_search` and Tavily work without approval. Probe the agent surface FIRST with a trivial prompt; if it fails, fall back to search-per-slice run by the orchestrator, and say so in the acceptance report — do not narrate a fleet that did not run. Second lesson: a chat session without filesystem access to the vault must deliver a **package + PATCHES.md** (copy commands, index/log lines, verify steps) rather than claim vault writes. Third: one primary full-text fetch (Hume) cost ~9k tokens of context; for primary texts prefer `scrapeOptions.formats: ["summary"]` or targeted `queryOptions` and keep the full text out of the orchestrator's window.
- **Same run, Law 7c confirmed on vault frontmatter:** all six pages were written with bare `[[wikilinks]]` inside YAML `related:`/`sources:` lists. A grep for the keys would have passed; `yaml.safe_load` rejected every file. Rule for vault writers: wikilinks inside frontmatter must be quoted strings (`related: ["[[a]]", "[[b]]"]`), and the frontmatter gate loads YAML, never greps.
